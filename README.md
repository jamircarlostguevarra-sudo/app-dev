# Favorite Movie: Inception

## Plot Summary
**Inception** is a 2010 science fiction action film written and directed by *Christopher Nolan*. The story follows Dom Cobb, a professional thief who steals information by infiltrating the subconscious of his targets.

---

### Main Cast
1. Leonardo DiCaprio as Dom Cobb
2. Joseph Gordon-Levitt as Arthur
3. Elliot Page as Ariadne
4. Tom Hardy as Eames

### Why It Is My Favorite
- **Mind-Bending Concept:** The idea of dreams within dreams keeps you guessing until the very end.
- **Visual Effects:** Practical effects like the rotating hallway fight scene still look amazing.
- **Musical Score:** Hans Zimmer's soundtrack builds incredible tension.

> "You mustn't be afraid to dream a little bigger, darling."

### Fun Fact
In the movie, the spinning top is called a `totem` and is used to test whether a character is in reality or a dream.
